{# The date-range picker shared by the cumulative screens. Two months side by side, because a window that crosses a month boundary is otherwise two clicks and a page turn. Every date is selectable - the point of a calendar is to ask for any window - and the days that have an upload behind them are marked rather than disabled, so you can see what will answer before you pick and still ask for something that will not. It stands on its own: it draws itself on page load with no data at all, and the host feeds it the marked days whenever they arrive. #}
